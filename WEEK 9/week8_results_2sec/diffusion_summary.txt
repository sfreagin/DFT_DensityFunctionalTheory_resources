Interpretation
------------------------------------------------------------
Use only a time region where MSD is approximately linear. Do not fit the very early ballistic regime.
Changing the fit interval is a useful way to see whether the reported diffusion coefficient is stable.
The subwindow spread is a pedagogical uncertainty estimate. MSD points are strongly correlated, so ordinary least-squares fit errors should not be interpreted as rigorous statistical uncertainty.
For publication-quality uncertainty, use multiple time origins and/or several statistically independent trajectories.
